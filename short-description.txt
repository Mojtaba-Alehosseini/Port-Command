Port Command Genova is a single-player strategy game in which the player runs the port of Genova as its Harbour Master. The multi-agent system, built in JADE, has seven kinds of agents (harbour master, terminals, tugs, customs, weather, assistant, and one agent per vessel) that communicate with FIPA-ACL messages; tugs win escort jobs through a Contract Net auction. Every binding decision is first checked by 30 Prolog rules on berth compatibility, escort, weather, customs and priority, so no unsafe move can be made. Walk-in ships negotiate the price and the berth time with the player in English: a Prolog DCG grammar reads the player's messages, a Rasa intent classifier handles what the grammar does not cover, and a small local language model (Phi-4-mini) explains the Assistant's advice, with a validator that rejects any explanation that contradicts the decision. The project was developed for the Symbolic and Distributed AI and Natural Language Processing courses of the University of Genova.
